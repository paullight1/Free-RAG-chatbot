# Incident response service-level agreement (fictional training data)

Priority 1 (P1) incidents must receive an initial response within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The incident commander updates stakeholders every 30 minutes until recovery.

Priority 2 (P2) incidents receive an initial response within 2 business hours. The support team must publish an internal post-incident review for any P1 outage within 5 business days.
